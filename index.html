Questions

COMMON QUESTIONS

Is DANDYAPP free to use?

Yes. Every tool is free, with no account, no sign-up and no ads.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. There is no server. Every tool runs as JavaScript in your browser tab, so tokens, client names and invoice totals are never transmitted anywhere. Your profile, clients, presets and documents are stored in your browser and can be exported to a JSON file.

Are the invoices SARS compliant?

The invoice template is built around the tax-invoice fields required by the South African VAT Act, including 15% VAT calculated inclusive or exclusive. DANDYAPP does not provide tax or legal advice, so you remain responsible for confirming that any document you issue is correct and complete for your circumstances.

Which currencies are supported?

Invoices and quotes can be issued in ZAR, USD, EUR or GBP.
