Free invoicing for South African businesses

DANDYAPP is a free invoicing app built for South African businesses. Create SARS-compliant tax invoices with 15% VAT, design the layout yourself, keep a customer book and see who still owes you. It runs entirely in your browser: no account, no sign-up, and your data is never uploaded.

Common questions

Is DANDYAPP really free?

Yes. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads and no limit on how many invoices you create.

Is the invoice a valid SARS tax invoice?

It includes the fields the VAT Act asks for and warns you while you type if something is missing, such as your VAT number, the invoice number, or the customer's address and VAT number above R5 000. You stay responsible for checking that every invoice is correct for your situation. This is not tax advice.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser, on your device. It is never uploaded, so nobody else has a copy. Clearing your site data or switching browser or device means starting empty, so download a backup regularly and restore it from Settings.

Does it work offline and on my phone?

Yes to both. After your first visit it keeps working without a connection, it is laid out for phones, and you can install it to your home screen like an app.

Can I use my own logo and fonts?

Yes. Add your logo and banking details in Settings, then design the layout in Templates with 13 built-in fonts or a font file of your own.
